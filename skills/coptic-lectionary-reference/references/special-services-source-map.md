## Search strategy that worked

When the ordinary lectionary sources were insufficient, useful search patterns were:
- `Coptic <rite> readings pdf`
- `Coptic <rite> service pdf`
- `Coptic <rite> readings`
- `site:... pdf` when a church or diocese host was visible in search results

Good recovery targets were PDFs from parish or diocesan hosts, especially pages that explicitly say:
- `readings`
- `rites`
- `service`
- `sacrament`

## Practical rule

Do not assume the ordinary Katameros contains every sacramental or special-service reading. For rite-specific questions, check this source map first, then search for the underlying service PDF or local workbook.

## Bidirectional lookup goal

The final lectionary reference should support both:
1. day/service -> readings
2. reading -> every day/service where it appears

For special services, keep the source and confidence level attached until the full reverse index is complete.
