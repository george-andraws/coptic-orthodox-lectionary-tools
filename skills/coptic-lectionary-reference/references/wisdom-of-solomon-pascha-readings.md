# Wisdom of Solomon Pascha-reading lookup precedent

Use this note when a Bible-study or lectionary task asks whether Wisdom of Solomon appears in Coptic Holy Week / Pascha readings.

## Verified checked-source placements

A Phase 1 Bible-study source probe for Wisdom of Solomon on 2026-06-03 confirmed these placements from checked Holy Pascha source text:

- Wisdom 1:1-9: Monday Sixth Hour of Holy Pascha
- Wisdom 7:24-30: Wednesday Eve Eleventh Hour of Holy Pascha
- Wisdom 2:12-22: Good Friday First Hour

## Lookup method lesson

For deuterocanonical Wisdom readings, do not rely only on the ordinary date-resolved or normalized passage query helper. If indexed passage lookup is empty or sparse:

1. Check the Holy Pascha / special-service data directly by day and hour.
2. Search source text for variants: `Wisdom`, `Wisdom of Solomon`, `Wis.`, and the chapter/verse span.
3. Treat source-text hits as checked-source evidence, not universal lectionary coverage, unless the reverse crosswalk independently resolves the passage.
4. Preserve day/hour granularity. Do not flatten these into ordinary annual readings.

## Wording standard

Good wording:

> In the checked Holy Pascha source, Wisdom 2:12-22 is assigned to Good Friday, First Hour.

Avoid:

> Wisdom 2 is read everywhere on Good Friday.

Avoid inferring placement from theme alone. Wisdom 2:12-22 is thematically obvious for the Passion, but the claim still needs source support.
