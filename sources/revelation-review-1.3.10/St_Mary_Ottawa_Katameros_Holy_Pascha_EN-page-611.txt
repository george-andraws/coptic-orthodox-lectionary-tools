The Ninth Hour of Good Friday
Glory be to the Holy Trinity our God unto the age of all ages, Amen.
The Doxology of the Ninth Hour of  Good Friday of Holy Pascha. May 
its blessing be with us. Amen.
“Thine is the Power…” on page 29 
The priests uncover their heads, and offer incense before the icon of the 
crucifix three times. and they prays the following inaudibly,
before His army, For His camp is very great; For strong is the One who 
executes His word. For the day of the Lord is great and very terrible; Who 
can endure it?
Priest:
First time:
We worship You, O Christ, and Your life-giving cross, that You were crucified 
on to deliver us from our sins. 
Second time:
O my Lord Jesus Christ, who was crucified on the cross, trample down Satan 
under our feet.
