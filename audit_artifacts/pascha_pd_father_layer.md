## Anchor assignment method

- `direct_psalm_exposition`: Augustine has a direct public-domain exposition on the psalm number surfaced in the row.
- `direct_reading`: the Father comments on the named biblical type or passage family, such as the bronze serpent or Passover.
- `typological_reading`: the Father comments on the biblical type carried by the row.
- `hour_theme`: the Father anchor bears on the hour theme when a direct passage comment was not surfaced in this pass.
- `overall` is `attested` when at least one of the three requested anchor types is present.
