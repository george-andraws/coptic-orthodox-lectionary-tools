## Youssef source extraction

- [read from source] ACCOT article, Chapter 1, section 1.1, under "Daily Readings and Sunday Readings," says: "For this reason, the Church has set up a special program of readings for the Sundays which differs from the weekday readings. Sunday readings are collected in the first volume of the Yearly Katameros."
- [read from source] ACCOT article, Chapter 1, section 1.1, under "The Program of Weekday Readings," printed page marker 32, says: "This program includes readings for every day throughout the Coptic year. If a day of the Coptic month falls on a Sunday, the rites and readings of that day follow a different program, i.e. the program of Sunday readings. The program of daily readings follows the Synaxarium: the theme of each day’s readings revolves round the occasions commemorated in the Synaxarium for that day, whether they be feasts of the Lord Christ, or our Lady the Virgin, or the angels, or the martyrs, apostles, patriarchs, ascetics and hermits, etc. There is a program for each kind of feast and commemoration. Accordingly, the Church has arranged 69 collections of readings to cover all these different themes throughout the Coptic year, known as the foundational readings."
- [read from source] ACCOT article, immediately after the preceding sentence, gives note 7 as "al-qirā’āt al-āsāsiyya" and then says: "They are collected in the second volume of the Yearly Katameros."
- [read from source] ACCOT article, Chapter 1, section 1.1, under "The Program of Sunday Readings," printed page marker 32, says: "Some of the weekday readings contribute to the message of the yearly program in co-operation with the Sunday readings. The weekday readings which play a part in the yearly program are the readings for the seven Major Feasts of the Lord and the seven Minor Feasts of the Lord, in addition to the readings for the two Feasts of the Cross."

- [inferred] The strongest basis for identity is the combination of source category, volume placement, function, and count: Ottawa is an English volume II weekday Katameros source with an annual mapping table and 69 dated reading sections; Youssef says volume two contains 69 foundational reading collections for those daily commemoration programs.

## What is established

- [inferred] The Ottawa/UKMID table of contents dated reading sections count to 69.
- [read from source] Youssef says the Church arranged 69 collections of readings called foundational readings, al-qirā’āt al-āsāsiyya.
- [read from source] Youssef says those 69 collections are collected in the second volume of the Yearly Katameros.
- [read from source] Ottawa/UKMID says the present book contains volume II readings and church feasts.
- [read from source] Ottawa/UKMID says many weekday readings are replaced by readings of other weekdays and points to the annual table that indicates this.
- [inferred] Ottawa/UKMID annual table uses the 69 dated TOC entries as reading targets for Coptic-year days.
