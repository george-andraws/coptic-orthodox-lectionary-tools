## Broad corroboration gathered this session
These are **corroborated themes**, not yet page-level extracted tables:

### Baptism
Commonly associated with:
- Psalm 51 / cleansing / repentance
- John 3:1-21 / new birth
- healing and anointing themes from James 5-type passages

### Crowning / Holy Matrimony
Important correction:
- the crowning service Gospel is commonly **Matthew 19:1-6**
- do **not** substitute the Cana wedding miracle unless the source explicitly says Cana
- Cana is a different wedding-themed passage and should not be conflated with the crowning Gospel

### Unction of the Sick
Commonly associated with:
- James 5
- Psalm 6
- Psalm 103
- healing Gospels such as John 5

### Myron / Altar Consecration
Commonly associated with:
- holy oil
- temple / altar consecration
- priesthood
- Exodus / Leviticus / temple dedication typology

### Bright Saturday
Commonly described as including:
- psalms
- prophecies
- psalis / praises
- the full Book of Revelation
- some Coptic practice descriptions also mention Psalm 151

## Practical rules for future work

1. Treat Bright Saturday as a **special-service bundle**, not as ordinary daily Katameros data.
2. Treat sacramental and pontifical rites as **service books**, not as annual lectionary days.
3. If a service is described only by family name in Coptic Reader, do not infer the exact reading table without page-level evidence.
4. For crowning, keep Matthew 19 separate from Cana unless the source explicitly names Cana.
5. If a screenshot or side-nav title is available, use it as the strongest clue for the exact reading reference before searching broadly online.
6. When a rite has multiple readings or hour-based subservices, search for the service page sequence rather than a single verse reference.
