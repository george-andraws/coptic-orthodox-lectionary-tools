# Obadiah: Synaxarium commemoration vs lectionary reading

Session learning from an Obadiah Bible-study lectionary check.

## Durable finding

Obadiah appears in Coptic liturgical sources as a Synaxarium commemoration, but current checked lectionary sources did not show Obadiah 1 as an assigned Scripture reading.

Use this distinction carefully:

- Toba / Tubah 15 commemorates **The Departure of Obadiah, the Prophet** in the Synaxarium.
- The readings for Toba 15 are prophet-themed, but they are not readings from Obadiah.
- Local chapter index state found during the check: `Old Testament, Obadiah, Obad, 1, Obad 1, is_read=no, occurrence_count=0`.
- Queries for `Obadiah`, `Obadiah 1`, `Obadiah 1:1-21`, `Obdias`, and `Abdias` returned no passage-reading hits in the checked local lectionary data.

## Toba 15 readings observed

## Recommended wording for future Obadiah study notes

> Obadiah is commemorated in the Coptic Synaxarium on Toba 15 as “The Departure of Obadiah, the Prophet.” The Coptic lectionary readings for that day are prophet-themed, but the currently checked Coptic lectionary sources do not show Obadiah 1 itself as an assigned Scripture reading. Local lectionary indexing records Obadiah 1 with zero reading occurrences.

## Workflow lesson

Do not collapse saint / prophet commemorations into passage readings. For prophet commemorations, check both:

1. Synaxarium/day title occurrence.
2. Actual liturgical Scripture reading rows for that day and reverse passage lookup.

If only the Synaxarium mentions the prophet, state that it is a commemoration, not a passage occurrence.
